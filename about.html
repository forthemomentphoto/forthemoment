A little about me · Upstate New York

Hi, I'm Amanda.

I'm a UGC creator and visual storyteller with a love for beauty, home, food and all the little experiments that make everyday life interesting.

Before creating content, I worked as a wedding photographer. That experience taught me to notice the details, work thoughtfully with light and composition, and find the story in a moment. It's a creative foundation I bring to every project today.

My content is polished but never too precious: useful product details, natural delivery, a little humor and the occasional kitchen mess.

When I'm not creating, I'm usually working on the house, trying a new recipe, finding a beauty favorite or planning the next place to explore.
