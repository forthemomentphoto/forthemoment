A little about me

An eye for the details. A soft spot for the everyday.

I'm Amanda Turner, a creator based in Upstate New York. Before creating UGC, I worked as a wedding photographer, where I developed a lasting appreciation for light, composition and the small details that make a story feel personal.

Today, I bring that visual foundation to beauty, home, food and lifestyle content — with a conversational voice, a little humor and room for real life.
